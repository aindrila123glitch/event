About The Hackathon

Built For Kolkata's Fiercest Builders

Organized by IEM, Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata.

🚀

Open Innovation Format

No artificial boundaries or mandatory sponsor tracks. Build what matters most to you—AI, web3, cloud architecture, system utilities, or creative tech.

⚡

Open-Source Code Sprint

Collaborate and contribute to open-source software, make impactful pull requests on GitHub, and unlock official developer certificates and digital badges.

🧑‍💻

12 Hours of Energy

8 intense hours of uninterrupted sprint coding supported by top mentors, midnight refreshments, lightning challenges, and live demos.

🏆

Jury & Venture Pitching

Present your deployed project in front of veteran industry engineers, founders, and academic leaders from IEM and premier tech organizations.

Registration Is Completely Free
