About The Hackathon

Built For Kolkata's Fiercest Builders

Organized by NEX Club, the technical innovation society of Dept. of CSE (AIML), Institute of Engineering & Management, Kolkata.

🚀

Open Innovation Format

No artificial boundaries or mandatory sponsor tracks. Build what matters most to you—AI, web3, cloud architecture, system utilities, or creative tech.

🎃

Hacktoberfest Celebration

Partnered with the global Hacktoberfest celebration. Make open source pull requests, push to GitHub, and unlock official swag and digital badges.

🧑‍💻

12 Hours of Energy

8 intense hours of uninterrupted sprint coding supported by top mentors, midnight refreshments, lightning challenges, and live demos.

🏆

Jury & Venture Pitching

Present your deployed project in front of veteran industry engineers, founders, and academic leaders from IEM Kolkata and premier tech firms.

Registration Is Completely Free

Ready to Hack on 31 October 2026?

Whether you are a seasoned full-stack engineer or submitting your very first Git pull request, AKI HACKS has a spot for you. Teams of 2 to 4 members.
