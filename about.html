Department Profile

Dept. of Computer Science & Engineering (AIML)

The Department of CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) at the Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata is at the forefront of technical excellence, cutting-edge research, and hands-on developer training in West Bengal.

Our students and faculty consistently push the envelope in foundational neural architectures, natural language processing, computer vision, and distributed software systems. Through curriculum projects and the PCCCS382 · IT Workshop course, we bridge theoretical computer science with high-impact software craft.

📍 Sector V, Salt Lake, Kolkata ⭐ NAAC 'A' Grade Institution
Student Organization

NEX Club · IEM Kolkata

NEX Club is the flagship developer and innovation society of Dept. of CSE (AIML), IEM Kolkata. Founded by tech enthusiasts, NEX fosters a vibrant peer-learning ecosystem through open-source contribution drives, weekly dev sprints, and flagship community hackathons.

With AKI HACKS × HACKTOBERFEST ’26, NEX brings together the sharpest coders, designers, and innovators across Kolkata for 12 hours of high-octane engineering.
