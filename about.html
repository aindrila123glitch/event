Institution Profile

Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM)

The Institute of Engineering & Management (IEM), Kolkata is at the forefront of technical excellence, cutting-edge innovation, and hands-on developer training in West Bengal.

Our students and faculty consistently push the envelope in foundational computing, intelligent systems, distributed architectures, and real-world software craft. Through hands-on laboratory workshops and curriculum initiatives, we bridge theoretical engineering with industry-grade development.

📍 Sector V, Salt Lake, Kolkata ⭐ Premier Technical Institution
Event Organization

Organizing Secretariat · IEM

The Organizing Secretariat at IEM drives premier student hackathons, open innovation sprints, and developer summits. Our mission is to foster a culture of experiential learning, open-source contribution, and high-impact software craft.

With INNOVEX ’26, IEM brings together the sharpest coders, creators, and builders for 12 hours of high-octane engineering and collaborative problem solving.
